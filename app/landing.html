THE TRUST BOUNDARY

Verifiable process.
Careful claims.

Emberline verifies that a configured approval process occurred against one specific evidence commitment. It does not claim that a blockchain alone proves a real-world event.

Read the implementation ↗
01
PRIVATE INPUT

Evidence stays outside the public ledger.

Files are hashed locally. Review happens through the project’s approved evidence channel.

02
SOURCE EVENT

Each decision is bound to exact context.

Reviewer, project, milestone, revision, commitment, and decision must all match.

03
USC VERIFICATION

Attestcoin bridges the proven Sepolia event.

The Creditcoin verifier checks the proof and accepts only the immutable registry event.

04
RELEASE POLICY

The escrow enforces quorum and sequence.

Duplicate proofs, self-review, disputes, and out-of-order release remain blocked.
